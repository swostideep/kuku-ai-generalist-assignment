How promos are chosen

Cut points never fall inside a spoken line, so every clip starts and ends cleanly.

Each window is scored on its hook (first 3 seconds), cliffhanger (the last line before the cut), standalone clarity, tension and pacing.

A clip must pass the hook, cliffhanger and clarity bars separately. A strong ending can't rescue a weak opening.

At most 2 promos per episode, never overlapping. The first 10 come from 10 different episodes, including the opening ones.
