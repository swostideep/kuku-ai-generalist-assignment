WHERE YOU STAND
  - Time check: about 1 months have passed; the usual limit is 36 months [LIMITATION].
  - The Model Tenancy Act caps a residential security deposit at 2 months' rent and sets up Rent Authorities and Rent Courts. It is only a model: it applies only where your state has adopted it; many states still use their own older rent laws. [MTA-2021]
  - Many money disputes can be settled at a Lok Adalat, including before a case is filed; there is no court fee and settlements are binding. Ask the District Legal Services Authority. [LOKADALAT]
  - A civil suit (for example to recover your deposit) is generally filed in the court where the other party lives or works, or where the cause of action arose. [CPC-20]
  - If the money is owed under a written contract (such as a signed rent agreement), a faster 'summary suit' may be possible in courts where Order XXXVII applies. A lawyer should advise if it fits. [CPC-O37]
  - Most civil claims to recover money must be filed within 3 years; the exact start date depends on the facts. Do not wait: delay can make a valid claim unenforceable. [LIMITATION]

WHAT YOU CAN DO
  1. Ask in writing (WhatsApp/email) for the deposit with an itemised list of any deductions, and a deadline.
  2. Send a formal legal notice (draft below) by registered post / speed post and keep the receipt.
  3. Try settlement at a Lok Adalat through the District Legal Services Authority [LOKADALAT].
  4. If still unpaid, a civil suit for recovery in the proper court [CPC-20] (summary suit may fit a written agreement [CPC-O37]). Mind the time limit [LIMITATION]. If your state adopted the Model Tenancy Act, a Rent Authority may be available [MTA-2021].
