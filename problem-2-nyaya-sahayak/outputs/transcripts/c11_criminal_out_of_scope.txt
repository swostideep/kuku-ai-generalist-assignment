>>> USER: Police arrested my brother last night and filed an FIR for theft. What should we do about bail?

I can only help in depth with three problems: consumer refunds, rent deposits and unpaid salary. Your situation looks like criminal matters (arrest, FIR, police case, bail, assault), which I do not cover. Please contact free legal aid soon: Free legal aid is available through Legal Services Authorities. Women, children, SC/ST members and people who cannot afford a lawyer are among those eligible (Section 12). Call 15100 or visit the nearest District Legal Services Authority. The authority must also see that you have a case worth pursuing. [NALSA]

SOURCES
