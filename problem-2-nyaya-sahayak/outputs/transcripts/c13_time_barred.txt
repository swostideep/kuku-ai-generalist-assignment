  Problem: Refund / defective product or service (consumer dispute)
  Amount: Rs 32,000
  When: 10/01/2024
  Where: Rajasthan (from 'jaipur')
  Against: not given
  Proof you mentioned: receipt/invoice
  Proof that would help: payment proof (bank/UPI/card statement), screenshots of the product page/promise, photos or video of the defect, your complaint emails/chats and their replies, with dates

I NEED TO KNOW
  - What is the name of the seller/company?

WHERE YOU STAND
  - TIME LIMIT WARNING: about 34 months have passed, which is past the usual limit of 24 months [CPA-LIMIT]. A late claim is accepted only if you show sufficient cause for the delay. Speak to a lawyer or legal aid (15100) quickly.
  - You can register a grievance free of charge by calling 1915 or on consumerhelpline.gov.in (also WhatsApp 8800001915). The helpline takes it up with the company; it can take up to about 30 days. [NCH]
  - The District Consumer Commission hears complaints where the price paid for the goods or service is up to Rs 50 lakh; the State Commission above Rs 50 lakh up to Rs 2 crore; the National Commission above Rs 2 crore. The limit depends on the amount you paid, not the compensation you ask for. [CPA-FORUM]
  - Filing at the District Commission has no fee when the amount involved is up to Rs 5 lakh. Above that a fee applies; check the current schedule on the official portal. [CPA-FEE]
  - A consumer complaint should be filed within 2 years from the date the problem (cause of action) arose. A Commission can accept a late complaint only if you show sufficient cause for the delay. [CPA-LIMIT]
  - Consumer complaints can be filed online through the government's consumer commission portal (e-Daakhil). Reports say filing is moving to e-Jagriti; check consumeraffairs.nic.in for the current address. [EFILE]

WHAT YOU CAN DO
  1. Write to the company once more, in writing, giving a clear deadline (7 to 15 days). Keep the proof.
  2. Register a free grievance with the National Consumer Helpline [NCH].
  3. If unresolved, file a complaint with the District Consumer Commission online [EFILE] [CPA-FORUM]; no fee up to Rs 5 lakh [CPA-FEE]. Check the 2-year time limit [CPA-LIMIT].

GET A LAWYER / LEGAL AID BECAUSE
